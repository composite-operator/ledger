SCORING CONTRACT

How GOAT ranks operators

GOATGains, Or Absolute Tragedy

01

Immutable original plan

Entry, stop, targets, thesis, and time horizon lock at publication. Later management never rewrites the original record.

02

Lifecycle discipline

Pending entries expire on an explicit horizon-based clock. A valid expiry or pre-entry cancellation applies a 0.10 discipline debit; technical voids are excluded.

03

Tranche-weighted outcomes

T1, T2, and T3 score by their declared allocations and exit R. Full exit at T1 and scale-out plans remain directly comparable.

04

Evidence qualification

GOAT stays NQ until three triggered setups resolve. Evidence weight grows with the sample and reaches full weight at twenty.

05

Outlier control

Each resolved trade contributes between -1R and +5R to GOAT. Full uncapped Trade R remains visible in total score.

06

Coaching, not execution

Review prompts can suggest a prospective Ledger stop. Only the author can revise it; the original stop stays visible and no broker action is claimed.

Trade R = Σ (allocation × exit R) Net Edge R = (Σ clamped Trade R − discipline debits) ÷ triggered resolved trades GOAT = 100 × (75% × Edge + 25% × Consistency) × Evidence
