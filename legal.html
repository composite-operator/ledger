Purpose and operating model

Composite Operator is a simple, unmonetized public record for traders. Its value comes from users earning credible track records through plans posted before their outcomes are known.

The current Services do not charge for access, sell placement, sell order flow, or sell personal information. Transparent records help traders evaluate ideas, learn from one another, and improve together. A rising tide lifts all traders.

Monetization is not the purpose of the project or an intended future goal. The operating model could still change if growth makes that necessary. If it changes, we will explain the change and update these documents before the new model applies.
